Open source · Runs on your machine · Nobody's business model
The AI that really does things.
Organizes your inbox, sends emails, manages your calendar, checks you in for flights.
All from WhatsApp, Telegram, or any chat app you already use.
Run it for yourself — or share it with your team.
No subscription. No hosted tier. No token. Stewarded by an independent 501(c)(3). Your memories. Your skills. Your models. Your machines. Your data.

installer pnpm
macOS & Linux Windows
β Beta
# Works everywhere. On macOS, first run may need an Administrator for Homebrew.
$ curl -fsSL https://openclaw.ai/install.sh | bash
# Install OpenClaw
$ npm i -g openclaw
# Meet your lobster
$ openclaw onboard
# For those who read source code for fun
$ curl -fsSL https://openclaw.ai/install.sh | bash -s -- --install-method git
# Source checkouts are pnpm workspaces
$ git clone https://github.com/openclaw/openclaw.git
$ cd openclaw && corepack enable && pnpm install
# Run from source; bundled plugins use extensions/* deps
$ pnpm openclaw onboard

What It Does
Runs on Your Machine
Mac, Windows, or Linux. Bring hosted, subscription-backed, gateway, or local models. State lives on your machine, not a vendor cloud.
Chat Where You Are
Talk to it on WhatsApp, Telegram, Discord, Slack, Signal, iMessage, or any of its 29 channels. Works in DMs and group chats.
Persistent Memory
Remembers your preferences, projects, and people from one conversation to the next.
Full System Access
Browses the web, fills forms, reads and writes files, runs shell commands. Full access or sandboxed—your choice.
Works With Your Team
Share one gateway: sessions the whole team can open and steer, live presence, and commits credited to the people behind them.
Skills & Plugins
Extend with community skills or build your own. It can even write its own.
Works With Everything

Who is behind OpenClaw
Read the announcement related content
OpenClaw is stewarded by the OpenClaw Foundation , an independent US 501(c)(3) non-profit. The Foundation employs the core team, signs every release, and keeps the whole product MIT licensed. There is no enterprise edition and no paid version.
The Foundation is funded by donors, including the University of Michigan, OpenAI, Amazon, Red Hat, Offline Holdings, and Lobster Computer Company. Donors fund the Foundation. They do not own, control, or direct the project, and no lab's model is privileged in the code. OpenAI is a donor and Peter Steinberger works there; OpenClaw is not an OpenAI product.
We have nothing to sell you, so we have no reason to route you anywhere. By default an install sends the Foundation nothing but a daily version check, and you can turn that off: what OpenClaw sends .
Donors and sponsors
Donors and infrastructure sponsors support the Foundation; none of them own or direct the project. Also supported by Amazon, Red Hat, the University of Michigan, Offline Holdings, and Lobster Computer Company.
