Can I share the AI benefits included in Google AI plans?
Family plan members on a Google AI Pro plan can enjoy AI benefits and features at no extra cost. Learn more .
I’m a Google Workspace customer, can I sign up for Google AI plans?
Only personal Google Accounts can sign up for Google AI plans. Workspace customers can sign up for a Gemini add-on to their existing subscription. Learn more about which offering works best for you and your business.
What countries are Google AI plans available in?
Google AI Plus is available in over 160 countries. Google AI Pro is available in over 150 countries. Google AI Ultra is available in more than 150 countries — see full list of countries . Certain AI benefits are only available for ages 18+. Learn more .
Gemini app and Gemini in Gmail, Docs, and more in Google AI plans are only available for ages 18+. Gemini in Gmail, Docs, and more is available in select languages — see full list of languages .
How do I use the AI benefits included with Google AI plans?
When you start a trial or are subscribed to a Google AI plan, you can use the following AI features:
Gemini app: Access it at gemini.google.com or through the mobile app .
Generate AI videos in Google Flow and the Gemini app.
Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Slides, Sheets, and Meet: Capabilities can be used directly within these Google products.
Gemini Notebook: Access it at notebook.google .
Can I create videos with a Google AI plan?
Our Google AI plans give you access to video generation in Gemini and Google Flow to help you bring your ideas in motion.
What is the difference between AI Premium and a Google AI Plus plan?
The Google AI Premium plan has a new name: Google AI Plus. It includes the same benefits like the Gemini app, Gemini Notebook, 400 GB of storage, and more.
