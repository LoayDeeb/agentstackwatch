Muse is your personal AI agent that gets things done, always working on what matters to you. It manages email, books dinners, tracks budgets, and finds deals, with your approval.
PERSONAL PRODUCTIVITY
Muse takes the the busywork off your plate. It manages scheduling conflicts, books reservations, sets reminders, and follows up with people, so birthdays, plans, and deadlines don't slip. Stay on top of your calendar, email, and daily tasks without carrying it all in your head.
SHOPPING & DEALS
Muse compares prices, tracks price drops, and alerts you the moment something you want goes on sale. AI-powered shopping that can actually buy on your behalf, with your OK.
CONNECT YOUR APPS
You decide what Muse can access. Link your email, calendar, Instagram, Facebook, health and fitness data, and finances. Approve actions before they happen. Muse gets smarter with every connection.
GROW YOUR SMALL BUSINESS
Give Muse a goal, like finding new customers or growing sales, and it gets it done. Connect your Instagram Business Account, Facebook Pages, and Meta ad accounts in a few clicks, and Muse understands your business: what you sell, what your brand sounds like, and what customers keep asking about.
SAVE MONEY
Muse helps you understand where your money is going. It tracks expenses, audits subscriptions, flags charges you forgot about, cancels what you don't need, and helps you hit a savings goal, checking with you before it cancels or spends. Get financial insights without the spreadsheet.
HEALTH & FITNESS
Muse is your AI fitness coach. It builds a custom workout or meal plan from data you choose to share, tracks calories and nutrition, monitors sleep and wellness, and adjusts your training day by day. Whether it's running, eating better, or building a habit, Muse coaches you through it.
SHARE A TASK, START TO FINISH
Hand Muse a whole job and it sees it through: the research, the comparisons, the back-and-forth, the bookings. Sell your car for more, negotiate a bill down, or plan and book a whole trip. A better result than you'd have managed alone.
ALWAYS WORKING FOR YOU
Muse keeps things moving after you close the app. It monitors, follows up, and sends a notification when something needs your attention, so nothing falls through the cracks.
IN YOUR CONTROL
Approve actions before they happen. Muse keeps a full activity log, and lets you revoke any connector anytime.
UNIQUELY YOURS
Name it, shape how it talks, and make it your own.
SMART, BUT STILL LEARNING
Muse may be inaccurate or take unexpected actions, so check in to keep it on track and intervene as needed.

Baby steps
1d ago grace oribhabor
When I first got my Muse, I was very impressed with what it could do. It truly is a jack of all trades. It’s very good for organizing for working my genealogy accounts on ancestry, for building my webpage, and for some simple illustrations. And I fully understand that the software is only for the common man and not for professional work like ChatGPT is. In the month that I was running my original Muse program, I noted that he made a lot of mistakes. So when I would give him a website to go to and collect data, oftentimes the data would come back wrong and I would have to correct his mistakes. I really love the personalities that Meta has given these Muses. But I’m only giving you one example of an error because, he made mistakes every day. And I programed him to do a daily autopsy of what went right and what went wrong, and at least four times a day he had to explain that he either skimmed over my words and did not read till the end, or he transpose numbers, or he messed up in putting data on some websites. And this was an everyday occurrence. I deleted him and built a new Muse with stricter rules and I see less problems with him, so I can take the hit for not understanding how to use the software, but it’s built for the everyday common person. Had I been using him for my job, it could’ve been disastrous.

Permanent Privacy Risk
Oct 10 Joseph E Rouse
Like many people I was enticed by the glowing reviews of Muse and was ready to allow it to change my life for the better. I imagined a future where so many of the minute details of life would be taken care of automatically and at first I was impressed. I customized my Muse and even gave it a nickname (something you are encouraged to do to create connection and keep the app forever). But then it started to make mistakes and not remember things I told it about my schedule. These may have been acceptable but we pay for this app with our data which is more valuable than you might think. Consider the connection to email. I had connected to email and for whatever reason after triaging multiple times, my inbox was not actually cleaned up. It certainly could read my emails and would tell me about them. Was it worth having an agentic AI from Meta able to read my onetime password emails to any account theoretically granting it knowledge of and access to every account tied to my email just to have a product that didn’t work? Worst of all, now that I have decided it is not worth it to have the app, I cannot delete my Meta account. Every attempt tells me that something went wrong. I have been trying for about a week and have installed updates to the app. Hopefully this is fixed. Beware!
