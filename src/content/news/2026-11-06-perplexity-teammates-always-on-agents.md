## What happened

In early **November 2026**, Perplexity launched **Teammates**: personal agents that take on **ongoing** work instead of one-off prompts ([Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/blog/teammates-are-agents-for-complex-ongoing-work); changelog dated [November 6](https://www.perplexity.ai/changelog/teammates-fully-local-computer-on-mac-and-voice-mode)).

According to Perplexity:

- A teammate **plans work**, runs tasks (including in parallel), remembers project goals and past feedback, and brings finished work back for review.
- Teammates can **monitor connected tools** (examples include Slack, GitHub and Gmail), start work when something changes, and ask for approval when they need a human call.
- Each teammate runs in its **own isolated desktop and browser**, building on Perplexity Computer, with separate browser sessions and encrypted saved credentials.
- Colleagues can hand a teammate work in Slack; teammates can also pass findings to each other.
- Availability: **research preview for individual Max subscribers** on the web (iOS soon). Free and Pro aren't included; Enterprise Pro/Max aren't in by default ([Help Center](https://www.perplexity.ai/help-center/en/articles/2026102601-perplexity-teammates)).

Perplexity's own examples cover marketing campaigns, sales follow-ups, support triage and ops checklists. Coverage put the Max plan at about **$200 a month** ([The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/1028302/perplexity-teammates-ai-agents-work); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2308870/perplexity-teammates-ai-agents-for-ongoing-work/)). That puts Teammates next to OpenAI's **dots** and Anthropic's **Claude Duet** in the always-on personal-agent lane.

## Our take

**The product shape is settling: one chat that waits for you is losing to agents that keep going.** Dots, Duet and now Teammates all sell the same promise — give it a job, walk away, review the draft. Perplexity's twist is tying that to Computer (desktop + browser) and to Slack, so the agent lives where work already happens.

**For founders and business leaders:**

- **Only try this if you already pay for Max, or you're ready to.** At ~$200/month this is a power-user and small-team tool, not a casual trial. Start with one narrow job that already burns your mornings: inbox triage with drafts, competitor watch with a weekly summary, or "flag support threads that need a human."
- **Keep a human gate on money, messages and deletes.** Perplexity's examples include drafting outreach and refunds — useful only if the teammate must check with you before sending.
- **Compare the job, not the brand.** Ask: does this teammate replace a VA hour, a Zapier stack, or nothing yet? If you already use ChatGPT dots or Claude Duet, run the same recurring task on both for a week and keep the one that needs fewer corrections.

**For everyday users:** unless you're on Max, this isn't for you yet. Free and Pro still get Computer-style one-off help; Teammates are the always-on layer on top. When a cheaper plan gets something similar, start with read-and-draft jobs before anything that posts or pays in your name.
