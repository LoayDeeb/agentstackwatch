## What happened

On October 20, Matt Palmer, who works on Grok Bot at xAI, posted a five-minute "What's new in Grok Bot" video listing a dozen changes, including X DMs, tagging @bot, new connectors, voice calls and an Android assistant ([X post](https://x.com/mattyp/status/2112550158885917122)). The post had passed a million views by October 21.

According to Tesla North's write-up of the video, the headline changes are:

- **X DMs.** Grok Bot can read and reply to your X Chat DMs. Palmer said there are limits in place and "you can't spam people" ([Tesla North](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/20/grok-bot-x-dms-android-assistant/)).
- **Tag @bot.** Mention @bot in an X thread and your own Bot takes the request, once you have installed and set up the X plugin ([Tesla North](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/20/grok-bot-x-dms-android-assistant/)).
- **Android.** Grok Bot can be set as the phone's default digital assistant ([Tesla North](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/20/grok-bot-x-dms-android-assistant/)).
- **Smaller upgrades.** Voice calls remember the previous call for 30 minutes, and new connectors include Robinhood, Coinbase, Trello, PostHog, beehiiv and eToro ([Tesla North](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/20/grok-bot-x-dms-android-assistant/)).

The official changelog for version 0.71.2 (October 14) separately lists tagged email addresses such as name+shop@, where a routine runs only for that tag's mail ([xAI changelog](https://x.ai/changelog/bot)).

Whether the Bot needs your approval before each DM reply goes out is **unknown**: Tesla North says Palmer didn't say ([Tesla North](https://teslanorth.com/2026/10/20/grok-bot-x-dms-android-assistant/)).

## Our take

Reading DMs is useful. Replying to them in your name is a different level of trust. A wrong email can be explained; a wrong DM to an investor, customer or journalist on a public network is harder to take back.

**Who should care:** founders and creators who live in X DMs, and anyone weighing Grok Bot against ChatGPT dots or Claude as their always-on assistant.

**What to do now:** connect DMs if they eat your time, but start with read-and-summarize and drafts you approve, not automatic replies. Turn on auto-replies only for low-stakes threads after a week of checking its drafts. If you hand it your Android assistant slot, remember it can read what's on your screen.
