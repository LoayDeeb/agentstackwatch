## What happened

On **October 30**, Anthropic's official Claude account announced new names for its two top personal plans ([Claude on X](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2116220869479428500)):

- **Max 5x → Max**, still **$100 a month**
- **Max 20x → Ultra**, still **$200 a month**

"If you're a subscriber, there's nothing you need to do," the post says.

**The bigger change:** Anthropic is "removing the 5-hour limit for verified Ultra accounts, starting today with most existing subscribers." Newer accounts keep a temporary limit until they're verified, which Anthropic says "helps us prevent fraud and abuse", and you'll get an email when it's removed. **Weekly limits remain unchanged** ([Claude on X](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2116220869479428500)).

For context: Claude's usage normally runs on a rolling five-hour window plus a weekly cap, and everything you do in Claude on web, desktop, mobile and Claude Code draws from the same pool ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). The five-hour window is the one that stops you mid-task and tells you to come back later.

**What each plan now includes**, according to Anthropic's pricing page ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)):

- **Pro:** $20 a month ($17 a month billed annually).
- **Max ($100):** 5x Pro usage every 5 hours and every week, Anthropic's top model Fable for up to half your weekly limit, priority access at busy times, and $100 a month in Claude Platform API credits.
- **Ultra ($200):** everything in Max, **no 5-hour limit** for verified accounts, **10x Pro usage every week**, and $200 a month in API credits.

By Saturday morning (Amman time) the post had more than 5 million views and over 28,000 likes on X.

It lands a day after OpenAI went the other way on its own top plans: on October 29 it doubled the Work and Codex allowance on ChatGPT Pro 100 and Pro 500, and canceled a cut to Pro 200 it had announced in September ([ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes)).

## Our take

**This is a fight over heavy users, and you win either way.** Both big assistant makers spent the week giving their $100–200 customers more room. If you pay for one of these plans, check what you actually have now before you renew or switch.

**For founders and business leaders:** the five-hour limit was the annoying one. It's what kills a long afternoon of work: a big research job, a 40-page proposal, a pile of contracts to compare, or an agent cleaning up your CRM export. On Ultra that wall is gone, but the **weekly** cap isn't, so a few long, uninterrupted days could use up a week's allowance. Plan your big jobs around that. And note the plan is personal: for a team, Claude's Team plan with Premium seats is usually the better buy; we break that down in [ChatGPT vs Claude for business](/chatgpt-vs-claude-for-business/).

**Is $200 worth it?** Only if you regularly get cut off on Max or Pro in the middle of real work. If you hit limits once a week, stay on Max at $100 and buy usage credits when you need them; Anthropic lets paid plans keep working at API rates after a limit ([Claude pricing](https://claude.com/pricing)). If you're comparing all the options side by side, our [How much does an AI agent cost?](/how-much-does-an-ai-agent-cost/) page is updated with the new names.

**For everyday users:** nothing changes on Free or Pro. Your $20 plan still has both limits. If you've never hit a limit, you don't need either of the big plans.

**One thing to watch:** "verified" isn't explained in the post. If you just signed up for Ultra and still see a 5-hour limit, that's expected; Anthropic says you'll get an email when it's lifted. These are Anthropic's claims; we haven't run a long session on Ultra yet.
