## What happened

At an OpenAI dinner in New York on **Monday, November 2, 2026**, ChatGPT consumer product lead **Adam Fry** told Big Technology's Alex Kantrowitz that:

- ChatGPT grew from **1 billion to 1.2 billion weekly users** in about 60 days.
- ChatGPT's volume of "search-like queries" is now **roughly one-fifth** of Google's.
- People create **1.5 billion images** a week, and voice (dictation plus talking to ChatGPT) has **250 million** weekly users.
- OpenAI plans to merge ChatGPT's **Chat and Work** modes into one.

All of this is from the interview ([Big Technology](https://www.bigtechnology.com/p/openai-chatgpt-search-is-about-20)). Fry described a "search" as the kind of question people used to take to a search engine, such as looking up a person, place or product, or finance and health questions where ChatGPT goes to the web. Put the other way round, Google still handles about five times as many searches, by OpenAI's own estimate ([Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/googles-search-volume-is-about-5x-chatgpts-openai-says/595817/)). These are internal OpenAI figures, not an audited market share.

## Our take

**Don't fight about the exact number. Watch the direction.** OpenAI counted its own searches its own way, so "20% of Google" is a headline, not a fact you'd bet a budget on. But 200 million new weekly users in two months is hard to fake, and it matches what most of us see: people now ask ChatGPT where they used to Google.

**For founders and business leaders:**

- **Ask ChatGPT about your own business today.** Type the questions a customer would ask, like "best [your product] for [your type of customer]" or "is [your brand] any good?", and see whether you're named, and who's named instead. If you're missing, that's lost leads nobody is reporting to you.
- **Give assistants something to quote.** Clear pricing pages, plain-language comparisons and real reviews on sites that rank are what these tools pull from. A slick homepage with no facts on it gets skipped.
- **Keep Google too.** By OpenAI's own math, Google is still about five times bigger. This is a second front, not a replacement.

**For everyday users:** ChatGPT is great for comparing options fast, but for anything about money, health or a big purchase, click through to the sources it cites before you act. The answer is only as good as the pages behind it.
