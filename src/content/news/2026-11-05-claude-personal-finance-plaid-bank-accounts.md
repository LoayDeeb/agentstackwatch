## What happened

On **November 5, 2026**, Anthropic opened a **Personal Finance** beta in Claude ([Anthropic](https://claude.com/resources/articles/introducing-personal-finance-in-claude)). Eligible **US** users on **Pro, Max and Ultra** can connect bank, card, loan and investment accounts through **Plaid**, then ask Claude about balances, spending and upcoming bills in plain language ([Claude Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18185620-use-personal-finance-in-claude); [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/claude-can-now-analyze-your-spending-with-its-new-personal-finance-tool/)).

The important limits, from Anthropic:

- Access is **read-only**. Personal Finance "can't move, manage, or invest your money," and it "isn't designed to give investment advice."
- Claude sees, for the accounts you choose: the bank's name, account name/type, last four digits, balances and credit limits, and **up to two years of transactions**.
- You sign in to your bank yourself through Plaid, so Claude doesn't get your bank password.
- Chats in the Personal Finance hub aren't used to train Anthropic's models.
- It isn't on Free, Team or Enterprise yet, and it isn't available in Claude Duet. Rollout is gradual, so you might not see it yet ([Help Center](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/18185620-use-personal-finance-in-claude)).

Anthropic's examples include questions like "Where did my money go this month?", "Am I spending more than I make?", and spotting duplicate charges or a bill that's crept up. ChatGPT already offered a similar Plaid-based money feature earlier in 2026 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/05/claude-can-now-analyze-your-spending-with-its-new-personal-finance-tool/)).

## Our take

**Your assistant just got a real view of your cash flow.** Chatbots have always guessed about "budgets." Connecting the accounts turns that into numbers from your actual banks — which is useful, and a bigger trust ask than connecting Gmail.

**For founders and business leaders:**

- **Try it on personal spending first, not the company card.** Read-only is the right starting point. Keep payroll, vendor payments and anything that moves money out of this experiment until you've seen how accurate the summaries are.
- **Ask the same questions you'd ask a bookkeeper.** "Which subscriptions am I paying for?", "What hits before payday?", "Which fees showed up twice?" Then click into the transactions Claude cites and check one or two against your bank app.
- **If you sell to consumers, watch this category.** Claude and ChatGPT are both becoming places people ask "can I afford X?" Clear pricing pages and plain answers about what you cost matter more when an assistant is summarizing your offer next to a user's real balance.

**For everyday users:** connect one low-stakes account first (a secondary card, not your main checking). Ask Claude which transactions sit behind any total it gives you, and confirm big numbers with your bank before you change spending or cancel anything. Unlink anytime if it doesn't feel right — Anthropic says Claude then loses that account's data.
