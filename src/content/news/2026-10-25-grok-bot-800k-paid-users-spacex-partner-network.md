## What happened

**The users.** On SpaceX's third-quarter earnings call, President Gwynne Shotwell said weekly paid users of Grok Bot climbed to more than **800,000** as of October, double the previous month's figure. She described it as an "always-on teammate" being used across software engineering, sales, marketing and IT operations ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/spacex-working-nvidia-apollo-other-175641418.html)).

**The partners.** On October 22, SpaceX announced the **SpaceX Super Intelligence Partner Network**, its official program for companies building products and services with Grok for business customers ([SpaceX SI](https://x.ai/news/partnernetworkannouncement)). It has two kinds of partners:

- **Transformation Partners** help companies deploy Grok across the business. They're being trained through a "Forward Deployed Engineer" accreditation program, with engineers trained at more than 30 organizations so far ([SpaceX SI](https://x.ai/news/partnernetworkannouncement)).
- **Marketplace Partners** build the plugins and integrations that connect Grok to tools companies already use ([SpaceX SI](https://x.ai/news/partnernetworkannouncement)).

More than 130 companies took part in early access. Named launch partners include Nvidia, Apollo, Snowflake, Databricks, Bain & Company, AHEAD and Galatiq ([AI Affairs](https://www.aiaffairs.com/corporate/spacex-grok-partner-network-early-access/)), plus Booz Allen Hamilton ([The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/news/spacex-grok-partner-network-apollo-nvidia)) and the consultancy CI&T ([CI&T](https://ciandt.com/us/en-us/announcement/ciandt-launch-partner-spacex-super-intelligence-partner-network)). Companies can apply at x.ai/partners ([SpaceX SI](https://x.ai/news/partnernetworkannouncement)).

**Still unknown:** SpaceX hasn't said what partners charge, how many Grok Bot users are businesses versus individuals, or how the user number is counted beyond "paid weekly". The partner list is heavy on big-company consultancies, and there's no small-business track announced.

## Our take

**800,000 paying weekly users in a few months is real traction for an always-on agent.** For context, Meta says Muse has passed 10 million users ([ToolSift](https://toolsift.ai/news/2026-10-24/)), but much of Muse is free, so the two numbers measure different things. What matters for you: there are now four serious always-on agents competing for your business (Grok Bot, Muse, ChatGPT's dots and Work, and [Claude Duet](/news/2026-10-24-claude-duet-anthropics-always-on-agent/)), and they'll keep adding features and cutting prices to win you.

**The partner network is the more telling move.** This is the same playbook Anthropic and OpenAI are running: AI companies have learned that big businesses don't adopt agents from a sign-up page, they adopt them when someone sits with the team and wires it into their tools. Expect consultants to start pitching "Grok rollouts" the way they pitched cloud migrations.

**If you run a small business, you probably don't need a partner.** A personal agent like Grok Bot, ChatGPT Work or Claude Duet is built to be set up by one person in an afternoon. Start with one painful, repeated task (weekly reporting, inbox triage, lead research), connect only the apps that task needs, and see if it sticks for two weeks. Our [small business guide](/best-ai-agent-for-small-business/) walks through picking one.

**If you're a bigger company and a partner pitches you,** ask three things before you sign:

1. **Which of our tools will it connect to, and with what permissions?** You want the smallest access that does the job.
2. **Who approves risky actions,** like sending to customers or touching money, and can we see a log of what the agent did?
3. **What happens if we switch providers?** Make sure the workflows and data you pay to build aren't locked to one model.
